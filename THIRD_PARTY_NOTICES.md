## Metro Manila boundary data

`infra/db/ncr_boundary.geojson` contains a simplified NCR feature from the geoBoundaries gbOpen Philippines ADM1 dataset (boundary ID `PHL-ADM1-36201628`, NCR shape ID `36201628B69282955287113`). The source geometry was simplified for this pilot.

- Dataset and metadata: [geoBoundaries PHL ADM1](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM1/)
- Original sources credited by geoBoundaries: National Mapping and Resource Information Authority (NAMRIA), Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), and OCHA Philippines
